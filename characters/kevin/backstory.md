Kevin only turns when the full moon falls on a Saturday. He plans his whole
life around it. The rest of the time he is a shaggy grey werewolf in reading
glasses, a patched wool nightshirt and a nightcap, holding a clay mug of mead.

He is tired. He is always tired. When the right Saturday comes he is
spectacular, briefly, and then he needs a lie-down.
