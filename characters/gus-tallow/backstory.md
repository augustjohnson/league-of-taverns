Gus owns two pockets. Both are stolen, from two different pairs of trousers,
and he has sewn them onto a vest that he also, technically, found. He keeps
a spare eyepatch pushed up on his forehead in case a situation calls for one.

His nose has been broken in three directions and points in a fourth. He looks
at most things sideways, which is either suspicion or the nose. He has never
been caught, and has been chased constantly.
