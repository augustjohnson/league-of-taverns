Mortimer requires a signed form before he will pass the salt. The form is
four pages. He has the forms with him at all times, along with a quill and a
wax seal stamp in brass to match his capped horn.

He is a small red imp with bat wings and a glare reserved for unsigned
requests. He has never once been rushed. He has been thrown across rooms,
and filed a complaint on landing.
