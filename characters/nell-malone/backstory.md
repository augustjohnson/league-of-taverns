Nell cannot hold her drink. It passes straight through her, beer foam and
all, and puddles politely under her stool. She orders another. She has never
once been drunk, and has never once been refused service.

A copper plate patches a crack in her skull from an argument with a door.
She is the most cheerful skeleton anyone has met, though she admits the
competition is thin.
