Uncle Amon is four thousand years old and still trailing linen behind him
everywhere he goes. He has one glowing green eye, a sheepish grin and
bandages that come loose on stairs.

He was a king once, or the brother of one, or the king's accountant. The
details have unravelled. He is everyone's uncle now and turns up at family
dinners nobody remembers inviting him to.
