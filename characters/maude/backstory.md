Maude hugs everyone. The healers have asked her, formally and in writing,
to stop. She has the letter framed. She is a brown-feathered owlbear with
huge amber eyes and a cracked beak tip, and she is always overjoyed to see you.

The heart tattoo on her arm was a gift from someone she hugged. She is still
looking for them to say thank you. Several people are keeping very still in
the hope that she won't find them.
