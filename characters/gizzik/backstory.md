Gizzik was named Rookie of the Year by Gizzik, at a ceremony he organised,
and presented himself with a trophy made from a dented tin bucket. He wears
it around his neck like a medal. He has not taken it off since, including to
sleep, which has not been good for his neck.

He has three rings in one enormous ear and a gap-toothed grin he aims at
everyone. He intends to win the award again this year. The judging panel is
confident he will.
