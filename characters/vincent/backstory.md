Vincent shows up uninvited at three in the morning, through the wall. He
cannot speak. He stays anyway. He brings a wilted flower and holds it out,
and looks at you with mournful eyes over a droopy pale-blue mustache.

He is a ghost and he is your ex, and he is not entirely sure which came
first. He has never once taken the hint. He has never once knocked.
