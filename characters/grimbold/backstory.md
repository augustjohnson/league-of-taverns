Grimbold has written a strongly worded letter to every mountain he has lived
under. None has replied. He takes this as an admission of guilt and keeps
copies, in triplicate, in a chest that is now too heavy to move.

He is ash-grey, with a long beard braided into tight knots, a quill tucked
in the braids and an iron helm he has never taken off in company. His scowl
is permanent. Nobody remembers what started it, including him.
