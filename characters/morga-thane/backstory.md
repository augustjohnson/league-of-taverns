Morga writes tender sonnets about the moon, its silver light and its quiet
distance. Every one of them rhymes with "smash". She has written two hundred
of them and is still finding new ways.

She keeps a quill behind her ear and small round reading glasses balanced
above a broken left tusk. When she reads aloud the room goes quiet, partly
from feeling and partly from proximity.
