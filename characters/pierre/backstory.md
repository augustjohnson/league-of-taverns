Pierre is probably a hat. He hangs on hat racks, black and floppy, with one
tentacle tip curled, and waits to be tried on. When nobody does, he takes it
personally.

He watches the coat room with shifty eyes from under his own brim. Guests
who do try him on discover several things about him at once. He has never
had a return customer.
