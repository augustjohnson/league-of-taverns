Hyekh!k'ka'a has never finished telling a joke. He starts laughing at the
setup, howls through the middle and is on the floor before the punchline,
which nobody has ever heard. People suspect there isn't one.

He wears a loud striped vest and a gold canine tooth that catches the light
when he laughs, which is always. A notched ear is the only souvenir of an
audience that wanted the ending.
