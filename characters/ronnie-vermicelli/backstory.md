Ronnie changes at the full moon and does a full set every time, stage or no
stage. He has slicked-back black hair, a chipped front incisor and a gaudy
maroon shirt open one button further than anyone asked.

As a rat he croons with his eyes closed. As a man he does the same. Audiences
have never been able to say with confidence which one they were watching.
