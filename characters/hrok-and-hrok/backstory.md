Hrök and Hrøk are two heads on one body and have been arguing for twenty
years about whose turn it is to scratch. The bald one wears an eyepatch. The
ginger one wears a beard. Both of them wear a grudge.

They are a grey-skinned ettin, thirteen feet tall, and in a fight they hit
very hard in two directions. They agree on almost nothing, except that the
other one started it.
