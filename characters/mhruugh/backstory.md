Mhʀ'ūūgh is the league's leading expert on labyrinths. He gets lost in
hallways. He has written the definitive guide to mazes, and could not find
the printer's to deliver it.

He is a hulking brown minotaur with a gold nose ring and one sawn-off horn,
and he squints at doors as though they owe him money. He has never once been
outsmarted by a maze. Corridors are another matter.
