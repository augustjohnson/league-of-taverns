Auntie Grizelda attends every family event uninvited, with a casserole
nobody should eat. Nobody knows whose aunt she is. Everybody calls her Auntie
anyway, because it seems safer.

She is green and warty, with a single long tooth, rag curls in her grey hair
and a smoking clay pipe. She knows everybody's business. She smirks as if
she knows yours.
