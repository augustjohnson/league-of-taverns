Vera has won every singing contest she has entered. The judges left early
each time, and the prizes were left on the table with a note. She has a
display case of them.

She has wild teal feathers for hair, a chipped beak and a battered brass
speaking trumpet she sings through because it makes her louder. It does.
She is always mid-song. Nobody has heard the start of one.
