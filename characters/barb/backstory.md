Barb controls every tavern bin within forty miles. Raccoons pay her a cut.
Foxes ask permission. She accepts tribute in pie crusts and wears a crushed
wicker bread basket as a crown, which nobody has been brave enough to correct.

She has a white blaze on her chest and crumbs in her fur at all times, as
befits her station. Her stare is calm, regal and fixed on your sandwich.
