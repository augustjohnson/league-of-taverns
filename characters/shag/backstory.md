Shag's origin is unknown. It is a shaggy burnt-orange rug with tasselled
edges, two woven eyes and a large brown stain in the middle that nobody
explains. Nobody asks twice.

It lies very still in front of fireplaces and waits for someone to wipe
their feet. It has never been cleaned. It has been stepped on often, and
by most people only once.
