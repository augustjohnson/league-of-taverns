Jenkins gives directions to everyone, unprompted. He has never once been
right. He is a glowing pale-blue orb with a smug little face, and he floats
ahead of people in the dark, confidently, toward bogs.

He is helpful, he says. He knows a shortcut, he says. Several search parties
have now been lost while looking for people he helped.
