Lenore's tongue has caught flies, coins, a lute and, on two separate
occasions, the bard. She returned the lute. The bard was let go eventually,
with an apology and a gold hoop earring that she kept.

She is fat, green, yellow-throated and heavy-lidded, and she sits very still
until something interesting moves. She considers almost everything interesting.
