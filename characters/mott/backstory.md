Mott has been the first recorded case of four separate illnesses, and was
asked to sit for a portrait by the healers' guild for each. He has a torn
left ear, several bald patches and a nose that has not stopped running in
living memory.

He feels fine. He says so calmly, often, and at close range. Nobody has
ever been able to prove otherwise, and several have tried from across the room.
