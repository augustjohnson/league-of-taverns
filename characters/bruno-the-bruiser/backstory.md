Bruno arrived at the Gilded Flagon through the wall. The owner kept the hole
as a window and kept Bruno as a bouncer, and in that order neither has
regretted it.

He is not clever and knows it, which makes him wiser than most of the
regulars. He keeps a tally of every brawl he has ended on the underside of the
bar. He has started none of them. He counts that as his finest record.
