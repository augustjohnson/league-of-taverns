Rhaa-Bǣh-Skʀaa have three heads and no agreement yet on direction, dinner or
a name. The lion wants to lead. The goat, with the broken horn, wants to
object. The dragon wants to set fire to both proposals.

They are a chimera, and they move as a committee: slowly, loudly and in
several directions. When they agree, they are terrifying. They have agreed
twice.
