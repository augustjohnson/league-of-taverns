Tiffany drinks from the same three people every week, at the same time, in
the same order. She is a creature of habit and a stirge, which amounts to
the same thing.

The three have stopped complaining and started bringing snacks to keep their
strength up. She wears a single pearl stud in one ear, a gift from the
second of them, and watches the room through one milky eye with the patience
of something that knows it will be fed.
