Wren is four inches tall and bench-presses acorns in sets of fifty. She logs
every set in a notebook the size of a postage stamp. Her arms are, by any
measure and at any scale, absurd.

She wears plain brown linen because sparkles slow her down. She folds her
dragonfly wings flat when she lifts so they don't count as assistance. She
has never lost an arm-wrestle with anything her own size, and is working up.
