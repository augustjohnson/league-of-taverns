Randall has attended four hundred weddings and been invited to none. He
arrives with a wreath of wilted flowers, a tin whistle in his mouth and a
gift that is always somebody else's.

One of his horns curls and the other is a stub, broken during the toasts at
wedding two hundred and twelve. He winks at the bride. He winks at the
groom. He stays for the cake and is usually gone before anyone thinks to ask.
