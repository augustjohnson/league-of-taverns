Gary died at his desk in 1102 and has not missed a day of work since. He is
grey-green, short one ear, and has ink on fingers that no longer have feeling
in them. Accounting has not noticed any difference in his output.

After work he holds a dented pewter tankard, smiles politely and does not
drink from it. He has filed a request for annual leave every year since his
death. It is always approved. He never takes it.
