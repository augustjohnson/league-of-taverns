Helga has started a brawl in every tavern on the continent and finished most
of them herself. She is in the Hall of Fame, which is a wall in a cellar
where her name is carved over several others.

She has thick blonde braids, a missing front tooth and a scar through one
eyebrow. She carries a broken bottle, and has done since the second brawl.
She grins like it's the first.
