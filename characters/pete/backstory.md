Pete was built from eleven people. Four were left-handed, and it shows. His
arms do not match, his eyes do not match (one blue, one brown), and there is
a bolt in his neck that nobody can find the other end of.

He smiles a bewildered, lopsided smile and does his best. Some assembly was
required. Some was skipped. He is still finding out which.
