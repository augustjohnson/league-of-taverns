Big Linda hugs every teammate after every fight. The hugs are warm, sincere
and have a body count. She wears a white apron tied around her chest and
keeps a wooden spoon behind her ear for stirring and, occasionally, for
emphasis.

Her muzzle has gone grey with years of looking after people who did not
survive being looked after. She does not hold it against them. She brings
soup to the funerals.
