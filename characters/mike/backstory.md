Nobody remembers anything after Mike arrives. Everyone wakes up purple. Mike
is a large violet mushroom with drooping tendrils and small round spectacles
of smoked glass, and he is always, somehow, already there.

He smiles through all of it, blissed out and unhurried. Hosts invite him
anyway, because the parties are reportedly excellent. Nobody can say why.
