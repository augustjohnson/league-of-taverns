Lady Constance invoices the tavern after every fight. The line items include
lace, powder, "emotional armour" and a recurring charge for having been made
to look at the opposition. Every invoice has been paid, because nobody can
find the person who would refuse it.

She inspects the world through a lorgnette and finds it lacking at every
magnification. The beauty mark is painted on fresh each morning, slightly
higher than the day before.
