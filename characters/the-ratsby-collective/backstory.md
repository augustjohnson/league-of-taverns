The Ratsby Collective holds a binding referendum before every step it takes.
Turnout is excellent. Results are announced by the white rat on top, who
does not vote and has never explained why that is his job.

Crossing a room can take an afternoon. Crossing it in the wrong direction is
unconstitutional. Opponents who wait them out tend to find that the
Collective has voted, at some length, to bite them.
