Carmichael has never moved forward in his life. He goes sideways, quickly
and with conviction, and calls it a strategy whether or not anyone asks.
His one oversized claw is crusted with barnacles he refuses to have removed.

He wears a knotted cloth headband for reasons that are, he says, between
him and the headband. His record in head-on confrontations is spotless,
because he has never been in one.
