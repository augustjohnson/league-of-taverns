Oakley is three hundred years old and still the newest member of the team.
He is sensitive about it. He has a bark face, a bird's nest on his head and
a carved heart on his trunk with initials nobody will explain.

He moves slowly and smiles shyly and has opinions he will share in about a
decade. The birds have been with him longer than anyone. He considers them
senior.
