Deborah has served as chief steward to three noble houses and has refused
every order any of them gave her, in writing, with copies. The refusals are
filed by date. The houses are all still standing, which she takes as proof
that the orders were unnecessary.

She has a notch in her right ear from a disagreement she won and a red band
on one foreleg whose meaning she will not discuss. She is a mule. She has
heard the jokes. She has filed those too.
