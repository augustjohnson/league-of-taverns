Dolores does a flawless treasure chest, a passable barrel and an upsetting
wardrobe. She has brass corners, one of them missing, and wears red lipstick
on jagged teeth because presentation matters.

She prefers to be opened slowly. Her tongue is long and pink and has
introduced itself to several adventurers, who did not stay long enough to
introduce themselves back.
