There is no one inside Sir Nobody. He has never been more sure of anything.
His plate armour is dented, silver and spotless, with one deep dent in the
breastplate that he describes as a medal.

He stands with his chest out and his visor open on perfect darkness, a blue
plume drooping over the top. He gives speeches about courage. Nobody knows
where the voice comes from, and he would rather they didn't ask.
