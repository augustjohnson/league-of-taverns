Gloria has been mopped up eleven times. Each time she comes back a little
angrier and a little glossier. Someone left a mop head in her the eighth
time, and she has kept it as a reminder.

She lives near an overturned wooden bucket she regards as hers. She does not
speak, but she has a smirk that says she remembers which aisle it was, and
who held the mop.
