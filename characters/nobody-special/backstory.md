Nobody Special appears in every team portrait as a different person and
denies being in any of them. They are grey, smooth and featureless, and wear
an obviously fake black mustache, slightly crooked, as a disguise.

They have been a guard, a bard and, briefly, the mayor. Nobody noticed. They
stand stiffly and look innocent, and you are certain you have met them before.
