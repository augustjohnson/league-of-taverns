Vix owns eleven caves, by her own paperwork. The paperwork is a ledger she
carries under one arm and has never let anyone read. The bats pay in guano,
which she accepts, files and resells.

She lost the tip of her left horn in a boundary dispute with a cave that did
not, strictly, have an owner. She considers the matter settled. She still
sends it an invoice every spring.
